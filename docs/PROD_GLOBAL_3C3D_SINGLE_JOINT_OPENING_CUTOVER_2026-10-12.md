## 2026-10-12 PRODUCT OWNER CORRECTION — generic whole-game and MULTI-NIGHT strategic continuity (mandatory)

**This section supersedes narrow Poisoner/FT-event and Empath/FT-Drunk interpretations of later development work.** The **strategist** is one whole-game, multi-night planner; **role-/event-specific rules** stay inside Host legal-domain producers and observation semantics, and are NOT separate model planners.

- **Initial call:** from the entire seated roster, experience, show/actual constraints and legal script, jointly choose opening settings with a *multi-night view*. Evaluate possible continuations for **all relevant roles and interactions** (including repeat information, death-triggered information, protection, impairment, shifting abilities, Spy/Recluse registration, possible claims, evil deception and the changing information network). Initial decisions must keep future plausible, not merely avoid immediate Good or Evil pressure.
- **Impaired roles:** picking **any** Townsfolk as Drunk affects the immediate information network and possible later story, even for roles whose ability is once-only, at night, during the day or activated on death. No dedicated “Empath Drunk memory”, “Fortune Teller Drunk series”, or per-role policy. In repeated-information cases the goal is **plausible causal and narrative continuity** across nights, **not** a hardcoded unchanging number, a guaranteed false result, or fabricating future queries.
- **Strategy memory:** `strategyMemory.tensions/intentions/uncertainties` is a bounded **private, defeasible** multi-night policy describing important seat relationships, conditional future trajectories, what may invalidate them, and epistemic uncertainty. Host keeps it separately from canonical observations/actions; a valid model response must preserve the memory in `HostOpeningSemanticAcceptanceV2` instead of parsing and discarding it. Subsequent calls receive both the *prior* policy and the latest Host facts. Compare each important earlier intention: KEEP, REVISE, or RETIRE with rationale. A manual Drunk override is an important fact and may reflect an operator preference **only if explicitly provided**; do not claim to know an unspoken human intention.
- **Generic change protocol:** **any material committed Host state transition, operator override, learned information, public claim recorded as a claim, action, death, nomination, poison, role change, or published clue** may trigger the same strategist-level `review → continue/replan whole unexecuted residual` route. Make this trigger independent of role and event type. For events with no material effect, keep the existing policy without API call; for changes that invalidate it, revise *all coupled unexecuted choices* in ONE global continuation, never invoke isolated “Drunk repair” or “Poisoner repair” model requests.
- **Different epistemic types:** confirmed Host actions, public claims, player-selected targets, storyteller manual decisions, provisional UI actions and AI speculative intentions remain distinct. Only committed facts can establish legality or published history. Already told observations are immutable. Unknown future events stay conditional. Rules-specific result matching is allowed **inside Host**, not as new orchestration branches or special-case AI prompts.
- **Testing:** vary both affected **role families** and **change types**; explicitly include a manual Drunk override, multiple-night observations with changes in role/poison/death context, claims that must stay claims, and a non-information role's downstream effect. Examples (including Empath, Fortune Teller, Poisoner) are evidence of generality, **not golden strategic answers**.
- **Status:** prompt and bounded-strategy-capture contract updated on current PR #304 branch; generic Host-change dispatcher and live continuation model call **not implemented yet**.
