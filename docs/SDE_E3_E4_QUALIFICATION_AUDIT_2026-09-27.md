## 4. Why Red Herring does not unlock C5

The C4 architecture audit already records:

`Evin GOLD — E1 yes, E2 candidate, E3 no`.

Evin directly supports:

- truth danger as a real feature dimension;
- credibility disruption as a real downstream mechanism;
- contextual Red-Herring setup commitments.

It does not authorize:

- always preferring the strongest information role;
- a Chef-specific preference;
- a scalar strength threshold;
- deterministic suppression.

Ben independently supports target ecology / likely Fortune-Teller targeting, but that is a different mechanism and cannot be collapsed into a fixed neighbour bonus or generic seat score.

Therefore cross-expert Red-Herring evidence validates context-sensitive descriptive features without providing one stable preference direction.

## 6. Exact next evidence requests

### Gap A — healthy-information balance

Need one independent experienced Storyteller TB case with:

- beginner/mixed table context;
- reconstructable setup + Night-1 bundle;
- explicit rationale that an alternative was rejected/softened because Good otherwise had too much or too little useful information;
- production-recoverable legal alternatives.

### Gap B — independent impaired-information believability

Need a non-Ben qualified Storyteller case where:

- a Drunk/poisoned result is chosen;
- another legal result is explicitly considered/rejected or clearly contrasted;
- rationale is about believability, continuity, detectability or coherent perceived world;
- ideally recurring multi-night information.

### Gap C — role-function exposure

Need:

- functioning Librarian or Investigator;
- Spy and/or Recluse interaction;
- at least one healthy legal alternative;
- explicit rationale about choosing/avoiding direct role-function exposure.

### Gap D — Demon bluffs

Need an experienced Storyteller explicitly comparing legal bluff triplets/sets with rationale around:

- claim burden/cadence;
- narrative-route diversity;
- collisions;
- support from misinformation;
- fallback/recoverability.

## 7. Stop rule

Do not spend reconstruction effort on an E3 lead once any required dimension is irrecoverable:

- qualified Storyteller identity;
- committed state;
- observed Storyteller-controlled choice;
- legal alternatives;
- explicit rationale mapped to a generic feature/predicate.

Such material may remain qualitative evidence but must not unblock C5.
